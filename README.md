                                  # detective_game
 This is a text-based detective mystery game. You play as a detective investigating the death of a performer named Mikasa
 inside the Grand Lyric Theatre. Your goal is to explore locations, find clues, talk to suspects, and finally identify
 the real culprit before your energy runs out. 
 
                                  # Main points of the game
1.Explore different areas of the theatre
2.Discover clues related to the murder
3.Unlock and interrogate suspects
4.Use the information to make a final accusation
5.Solve the case before your energy reaches 0
6. You win by accusing the right suspect.
7. You lose if your energy reaches 0.

                                  # Task:
The objective of this open-ended lab is to explore, design, and
develop a text-based detective game using the relevant programming
constructs covered so far. The game should simulate a detective
investigation where the player explore, interacts with suspects,
collects clues, and ultimately solves the case by identifying the real
culprit.

                                 # Gameplay description
The game starts with the player having 100% energy.The player is
given an output display message telling about a murder. Then the 
player is given choices to explore different locations. The player
can search a location during which he can find clues, solve puzzles
and talk to the suspects. Each suspect has unique dialogues that the
player must read carefully to find the real killer. The player can also
skip a location or research it. All this activities consume energy.
Player loses energy as the game progresses and will lose more for each 
wrong guess or decision. You have to find clues and suspects and in 
the end, make a final decision to find the killer.

                             # Locations
The game includes four locations that the player can 
explore and find clues in. These are 
•	Backstage
•	Dressing Room
•	Stage
•	Prop Room

                       # Clue Unlock Logic
•	Backstage - script - unlock Levi
•	Dressing Room - lipstick - unlock Sasha
•	Prop Room - gun - unlock Reiner
Each location provides new clues and new suspects.

                   #Final accusation
Make a decision in the end to accuse one of the 
suspects that you believe is the killer. The correct answer in our case 
is Levi.
